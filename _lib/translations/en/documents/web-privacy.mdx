the fm01 website or contact us.

We may receive information you provide directly, such as your email address
and message when you contact support. We may also automatically receive
technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device
information, requested pages, and security or error logs.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to provide and secure the website, respond to requests,
understand and improve performance, prevent abuse, and comply with legal
obligations. We do not sell personal information.

## 3. Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use necessary cookies and similar technologies for security,
preferences, and basic functionality. See the [Cookie Policy](/legal/web/cookies)
for more information.

## 4. Sharing and retention

We may use hosting, monitoring, communication, and security providers to
operate the website. They may process information only to provide services to
us. We retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes above,
legal requirements, or legitimate security needs.

## 5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete,
or restrict the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a
request. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity.

## 6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our practices change. Questions and privacy
requests can be sent to [support@fm01.bot](mailto:support@fm01.bot).
