
# Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how **fm01** uses cookies and similar technologies
when you visit the fm01 website.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.
They help websites remember your preferences, understand how services are used,
and provide a better experience.

## 2. How we use cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to:

- keep the website secure;
- remember your settings and preferences;
- measure website performance and usage; and
- improve our services and user experience.


## 3. Types of cookies

### Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for core functions, such as authentication,
security, and saving consent preferences. They cannot be switched off through
this website.

### Preference cookies

These cookies remember choices such as language, region, or display
preferences.

### Analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the website. They are only
used where permitted by applicable law and with the required consent.

## 4. Managing your cookie preferences

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking
strictly necessary cookies may prevent some parts of the website from working.
If this website provides a cookie preference tool, you can also use that tool
to change your choices at any time.

## 5. Third-party services

Hosting, security, monitoring, or embedded services may process technical
information through their own technologies. Those providers are responsible
for their own policies and only receive information needed to provide their
services.

## 6. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be
published on this page with an updated generation timestamp.
