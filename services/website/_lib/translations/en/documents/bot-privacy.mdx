
# Discord Bot Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how **fm01** processes information when you use
the fm01 Discord bot.

## 1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, we may process Discord user, server,
channel, role, and message identifiers; server configuration; command input;
and technical logs such as timestamps, error details, and request metadata.
We do not request or intentionally collect Discord passwords.

## 2. Why we use information

We use this information to provide and configure bot features, respond to
commands, maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and improve
reliability. We use only the information reasonably needed for these purposes.

## 3. Sharing and providers

We may share limited information with infrastructure, monitoring, and support
providers that process it on our behalf. We may also disclose information when
required by law or when necessary to protect users, the service, or our legal
rights. We do not sell personal information.

## 4. Retention and security

Configuration and operational data is retained only while needed for the
relevant feature, security, or legal purpose. We use reasonable safeguards,
but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Your choices

You can stop processing by removing the bot from a server, where applicable.
You may ask about information associated with your server or account by
contacting us. We may need to verify the request before responding.

## 6. Children and changes

The bot is not directed at children who are not permitted to use Discord. We
may update this policy when our processing changes and will publish the
updated version on this page.

For privacy questions, contact
[support@fm01.bot](mailto:support@fm01.bot).
